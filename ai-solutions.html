Agents

Split the work, then call tools

A useful agent is a loop: plan, retrieve, call an API, check the result, and stop or ask a person.

  • Plan: break a request into steps you can log and debug.
  • Retrieve: search your files or database before the model answers.
  • Act: tickets, CRM, calendar, or custom HTTP tools.
  • Guard: confidence checks and human handoff when it should not guess.

Roles we wire together

Retriever — vector or SQL search over your content.
Executor — tools and the final answer in your UI.
